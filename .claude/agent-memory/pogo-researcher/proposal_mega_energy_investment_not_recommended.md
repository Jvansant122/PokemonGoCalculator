Status: considered 2026-09-10, explicitly NOT recommended to build yet. Recorded so a
future pass doesn't re-open it without new evidence.

**The idea considered:** price "spend Mega Energy to push a '+'-move mega toward Super
Max" as a Power-Up-Optimizer-style budget candidate competing against ordinary
stardust/candy power-ups — same shape as the already-recorded "second charged move
unlock competes for the same budget" idea (IDEAS.md item 10), on a third currency axis.

**Why not now:** both effects that would make this worth anything in this tool's math
rest on `[unverified]`/contested numbers per MECHANICS.md's "Mega Level" section —
the Super Max CP bump (+2 effective levels, mechanism unconfirmed) and the "+" move's
"+10% per tier" power scaling (explicitly closed as unsourceable across three research
rounds, most recently re-attempted and re-closed 2026-09-10: "two community sites carry
it with verbatim-identical disclaimer text... one shared guess rather than independent
corroboration"). Building a costed recommendation on two stacked speculative numbers
repeats, in miniature, the exact failure mode this project was burned by with fabricated
stats reaching the species picker — a materially worse case than presenting a single
sourced-but-uncertain number.

**What would unblock it:** MECHANICS.md names the missing evidence explicitly — one
real in-game reading of a "+" move's displayed damage at a known Mega Level tier would
pin the real multiplier and retire the estimate. Re-open this proposal once that exists,
not before.

**If ever built:** would need a new per-slot Mega Energy input (`megaEnergyOnHand`),
round-tripped through `PowerUpOptimizerAssumptions` like the other resource pools —
flagging per CLAUDE.md's "every user-facing setting must round-trip" rule, since this
proposal implies a new Scenario field it does not currently have.
