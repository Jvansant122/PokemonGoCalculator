Researched 2026-09-09, overnight dodging deep-dive (answers Q5). Re-checked whether the
already-recorded MECHANICS.md "Unconfirmed: dodge damage may scale with remaining HP" note (a
Silph Road observation of surviving 8 dodged Paybacks where 4–5 was expected) has any follow-up,
confirmation, or refutation in the years since.

**Finding: none found, either direction.** Searched directly for Silph Road / r/TheSilphRoad
research threads on Payback damage scaling, dodge damage decreasing with repeated dodges, and
general "does dodge damage change with remaining HP" — all searches returned either unrelated
results (other games, unrelated Pokémon GO news) or no matches. The only repeated-dodge-related
finding from this whole research pass was the *dodge-stacking exploit* recorded in
[[fact_dodge_dec2024_window_fix]] (Nov/Dec 2024, patched) — which is a different mechanism (each
dodge attempt gets a fresh, un-nerfed 0.25 multiplier, and they compound because multiple dodges
land on the *same* hit) from the HP-scaling claim (a single dodge's multiplier allegedly changing
based on the defender's remaining HP). Do not conflate the two if this gets re-investigated — the
stacking exploit is confirmed-and-patched; the HP-scaling claim remains exactly as unconfirmed as
it was before this pass.

**Verdict: close this out as still unresolved as of 2026-09-09.** No new evidence surfaced either
confirming or debunking it. Future research passes should not re-open this from scratch unless a
new primary source (Silph Road itself, or a fresh player report) surfaces — treat prior "not
found" as the current state, not as "not yet tried."

## Engine status

Unchanged: **not modelled**, and per the original recorded guidance, should stay that way until
actually confirmed with a formula — an unconfirmed HP-scaling multiplier is exactly the kind of
unsourced number this project has been burned by before.
